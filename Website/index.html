About this experiment

Both courtyard blocks are a new model reconstructed from the official renders, the developer's site-plan video and newkomitas.am (18 floors, 1,620 apartments). It includes the stepped north corners, angled balcony trays, copper frames and framed balcony groups. Dimensions are measured from those images, not from architectural drawings (about ±5%).

The lighting is Blender Cycles for the true sun position in Yerevan on 20 September at 16:30, baked into the model and shown in the same colour transform as the photoreal stills. Glass keeps live reflections of a panorama of the real surroundings.

Neighbouring buildings, streets, parks and traffic routes come from OpenStreetMap data (ODbL). Building heights come from OSM levels where tagged and are estimated otherwise. Trees are procedural.

All apartment data is synthetic. Unit positions, numbers, areas, plans and available/reserved/sold labels are invented to demonstrate the experience. They are not property listings.

The model and apartment data are independent, so status changes can be shown without rebuilding the architecture.

This experiment is separate from the existing apartment and video project.
